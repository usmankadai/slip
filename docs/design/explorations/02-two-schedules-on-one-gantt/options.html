Exploration 02: two schedules on one Gantt

The Gantt has to show this update and the previous one on the same rows, so a planner can see what moved without reading two charts. Drawing every activity twice doubles the ink and, when most bars have not moved, says nothing with it. The treatment also has to survive three awkward cases: a bar that moved by a day (less than a pixel at month zoom), a bar whose previous position is off the edge of the view, and an activity with no previous position at all.

Each option draws the same ten rows: a large slip, a one-day slip, an earlier finish, no change, a duration that grew, a previous position off screen to the left, a new activity, a moved milestone, a critical activity that slipped, and one in progress. The dashed line is the data date. Switch the zoom to see what each does when a day is less than a pixel.
