Exploration 04: the proposed edit card

The assistant can propose a change to an activity: a new duration, or a constraint. It never makes the change. A planner approves or rejects it; approving reruns the schedule; both are logged. The card has to give enough before and after to approve with confidence, including what the change does to the key milestones, which the engine works out before anything is changed. It must not become a form.

Each option shows the same proposals, drawn at the panel's width. Switch between a change that recovers time, one that makes things worse, one that moves nothing, and one already decided. The figures are made up but shaped like the engine's preview.
