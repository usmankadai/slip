Exploration 11: relationship lines on the Gantt

Three ways to draw logic on the canvas Gantt beside the changes grid. The grid shows a window of a filtered, sorted view, and by default it is sorted by finish slip, not by logic. Activities that are linked are rarely next to each other, and most of an activity's links lead to rows that are not on screen. That is what makes this hard: P6 draws every link because its rows are in logic order; Slip's are not.

Drawn with 14 rows of the 1,000 activity fixture's shape. The activity drawer already lists every predecessor and successor as text, so the lines are for seeing, not the only record. Colours are placeholders from the tokens; the slip colours are not used for lines, since warm means late.
