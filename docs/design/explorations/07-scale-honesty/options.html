Exploration 07: scale honesty

A planner looking at the changes grid sees about 25 rows. The schedule has 350,412 activities, of which 4,812 changed. Three things have to be clear without asking: how many rows this view holds, how many are hidden and why, and where in the list the visible rows are. The scrollbar is the usual answer to the last one, and at this size it stops working: the thumb is at its minimum size whatever the list length, and one pixel of travel is hundreds of rows.

The first two are not optional. The UI spec says the count of hidden rows is always visible when a view is filtered. The options differ in where that is said and in what replaces the scrollbar's lost meaning.
