Exploration 01: showing slip in a grid cell

Four ways to show how far an activity's finish moved between two updates, at the grid's real row height. The rows are real: 17 activities from the generated 1,000 activity rail pair, chosen to cover large and small slips, no change, earlier finishes, a new activity and a finished one.

Options drawn by Claude for Usman to choose between. The decision is in the README beside this file.
