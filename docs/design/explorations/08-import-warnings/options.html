Exploration 08: import warnings

Three ways to report what Slip had to repair or leave out while reading a file, drawn at the real width of an import slot (layout A). The same six kinds of warning are used in each; the kinds and what Slip does about them come from the import spec. The counts are made up to match a 350,000 activity schedule.

Warnings never block the comparison. None of the options uses the amber and red of the slip scale: in Slip, warm means late, and a warning is not a late activity.
