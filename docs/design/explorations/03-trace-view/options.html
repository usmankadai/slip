Exploration 03: the trace view

A planner picks a milestone that moved and asks why. The engine walks back along the driving path and works out, for each activity on it, how much slip that activity added of its own (it took longer, or its logic changed) as distinct from the slip it merely passed on from its predecessor. The answer is usually a handful of activities out of a chain of dozens. The design spec is firm about the hierarchy: the ranked causes are the answer, and the chain is the evidence.

Each option shows the same trace. The first case uses the real figures from the generated 1,000 activity rail schedule: project completion slipped 67 working days, caused by four activities adding 20, 18, 16 and 13 days. The activities between them on the path, and all the names, are made up. The second case is made up to test the awkward parts: an activity that recovered time, and a tail of small contributors.
